02 / About

Analysis needs
something to show for it.

I’m a Dubai-based student interested in AI, robotics, and how systems behave under pressure.

My pattern is to take a messy problem apart, find the structure underneath, and build something I can test. That might be a moving prototype, a local tool, or a market hypothesis that needs a proper control.

The work here is also a filter against overthinking. I care about the reasoning, but I want it to end in code, a result, or an explanation someone else can inspect.

Find the pattern. Build the proof. Explain the thinking.
