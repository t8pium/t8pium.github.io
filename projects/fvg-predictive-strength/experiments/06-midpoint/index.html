Experiment 06 / Exact 50% test

Midpoint / consequent encroachment

A direct test of whether the FVG midpoint behaves differently from the midpoint of an ordinary matched zone.

Why a symmetric race?

Starting exactly at the midpoint makes the near edge and far edge equidistant. This removes the geometric advantage that would exist if the entry were closer to one boundary.

Procedure

  1. Wait for the midpoint of the real or control zone to be reached.
  2. Start measurement from the following bar.
  3. Race a return to the near edge against a move to the far edge.
  4. Mark native bars that contain both boundaries as ambiguous rather than inventing an intrabar order.

Instability

The effect was not stationary through time. On 15m, for example, the yearly difference ranged from about +8.07 pp in 2020 to around zero or slightly negative in 2024–2025, before becoming positive again in the partial 2026 sample.

Native-timeframe ambiguity was also material because a coarse bar can trade both boundaries without revealing which came first.

Conclusion

CE sometimes behaves differently from an ordinary midpoint, but the result is timeframe- and regime-dependent. The study does not support treating 50% as a universal deterministic reversal price.

Exact implementation

Matched sample

RNG seed 9917. Each timeframe samples up to 12,000 eligible FVGs. Each real FVG receives 3 synthetic controls matched on session, volatility regime, trend regime and time bucket, while preserving the real FVG's direction, ATR-normalized width and ATR-normalized starting distance.

Midpoint-touch window

The Numba midrace function searches for an exact midpoint/CE touch during the next 20 native bars. For bullish gaps, a low must reach the midpoint; for bearish gaps, a high must reach it. If CE is never touched inside that window, the event is not part of the subsequent barrier race.

Symmetric race

Measurement begins on the next bar after the midpoint touch. The near and far FVG edges are equidistant from CE, so neither outcome receives an easier price-distance target. The race lasts 10 native bars.

  • Near edge first = rejection success.
  • Far edge first = traversal failure.
  • Both on one bar = ambiguous and excluded from the resolved win-rate race.
  • Neither in 10 bars = censored.

Inference

The three control outcomes are reduced to a parent-level resolved success rate. Only parent pairs with a resolved real FVG and a finite control rate enter the paired difference. The paired difference is then clustered by New York calendar date and bootstrapped 500 times.

Year stability

The exact same resolved pairs are grouped by calendar year to show whether a full-sample CE effect is stable rather than driven by one historical regime.
