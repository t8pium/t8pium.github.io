Experiment 08 / Incremental predictive value

Distance, regimes & controlled model

A controlled test of whether FVG status matters after accounting for geometry and market state.

Logistic model

A 60-minute touch model used approximately 240,000 FVG/control observations with predictors for FVG status, ATR-normalized distance, width, volatility, trend, time of day, and direction.

Volatility & displacement

60-minute FVG advantage was about +0.56 pp in low volatility, +0.93 pp in normal volatility, and +0.32 pp in high volatility. Stronger displacement showed a modestly larger effect, reaching roughly +0.90 pp for the strongest bucket.

Direction & size

Bullish and bearish FVG effects were almost identical: approximately +0.57 vs +0.59 pp. Removing tiny gaps did not eliminate the effect; ≥0.10 ATR and ≥0.20 ATR filters remained around +0.70 pp.

Conclusion

FVG status adds some incremental information, but distance and market state explain much more. This supports using FVG as one feature in a larger model rather than as the main explanatory variable.

Exact implementation

Deep-1m state definition

This experiment uses a deliberately different, longer state horizon than the multi-timeframe matching script. ATR is a 14-bar mean true range. Trend score is the 60-bar close change divided by current ATR. Volatility ratio is ATR divided by a 1,380-bar rolling median ATR with 300-bar minimum warm-up.

Control universe

Controls are valid non-FVG one-minute timestamps outside the roll block and with at least 4,140 future bars available. Each sampled FVG is matched on session, New York hour, volatility regime and trend regime. The FVG's exact direction, width/ATR and distance/ATR are transplanted onto the control timestamp. Seed 42; five controls per FVG.

Distance strata

60-minute touch outcomes are grouped by initial distance: <0.25, 0.25–0.50, 0.50–0.75, 0.75–1, 1–1.5, 1.5–2, 2–3 and 3+ ATR. The farthest cell is reported but treated cautiously because its event count is small.

Other strata

Separate tables are generated for gap width/ATR, session, bullish/bearish direction, volatility bucket, trend bucket, displacement-body/ATR and calendar year. Minimum-size sensitivity repeats touch/midpoint/full-fill results for all gaps, ≥1 tick, ≥2 ticks, ≥0.05 ATR, ≥0.10 ATR and ≥0.20 ATR.

Logistic regression

The binary 60-minute touch dataset combines real FVG observations with synthetic controls. Predictors are is_fvg, distance/ATR, width/ATR, volatility ratio, trend score, direction, and sine/cosine transforms of minute-of-day. The frozen implementation uses LogisticRegression(max_iter=200, C=1e6); the FVG odds ratio is exp(beta_is_fvg).

Interpretation safeguard

The model is not presented as a causal estimator. Its purpose is narrower: determine whether the binary FVG label still contains incremental information after several obvious covariates are present, and compare its magnitude with variables such as distance.

Exact controlled analysis

  1. Run the deep 1m matched-control study and retain the matched real FVGs plus five-control sets.
  2. Use the 60-minute touch outcome as the common dependent variable for the subgroup tables.
  3. Stratify the matched FVG sample by ATR-normalized starting distance, ATR-normalized width, session, direction, volatility regime, trend regime, displacement-body size and calendar year.
  4. Within each subgroup, compare the real FVG touch rate with all controls belonging to those parent events.
  5. Repeat the comparison under minimum gap-size filters: any, ≥1 tick, ≥2 ticks, ≥0.05 ATR, ≥0.10 ATR and ≥0.20 ATR.
  6. Build one combined FVG/control modeling table for the 60-minute touch logistic regression.
  7. Model features are: FVG indicator, distance/ATR, width/ATR, volatility ratio, trend score, direction, plus cyclical sine/cosine encodings of time of day.
  8. Fit near-unregularized logistic regression (C=1e6) and report exp(beta_FVG) as the FVG odds ratio.

The logistic model is not used as a trading strategy. It is a diagnostic: does the FVG indicator remain non-zero once obvious state variables are present?
