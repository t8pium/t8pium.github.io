Experiment 08 / Incremental predictive value

Distance, regimes & controlled model

A controlled test of whether FVG status matters after accounting for geometry and market state.

Logistic model

A 60-minute touch model used approximately 240,000 FVG/control observations with predictors for FVG status, ATR-normalized distance, width, volatility, trend, time of day, and direction.

FVG odds ratio~1.079
60m raw probability edge~+0.58 pp
Dominant featureDistance

Because baseline touch probability is already around 90%, a 7.9% odds increase corresponds to only a small absolute probability shift.

When a target is extremely close, almost everything gets revisited. The FVG label adds essentially nothing below 0.25 ATR.

Session

  • Asia: +0.85 pp
  • London: +0.66 pp
  • NY premarket: +0.81 pp
  • NY AM: −0.08 pp
  • NY lunch: −0.13 pp
  • NY PM: ~0 pp

The FVG-specific effect was weak during core NY hours in this sample.

Volatility & displacement

60-minute FVG advantage was about +0.56 pp in low volatility, +0.93 pp in normal volatility, and +0.32 pp in high volatility. Stronger displacement showed a modestly larger effect, reaching roughly +0.90 pp for the strongest bucket.

Direction & size

Bullish and bearish FVG effects were almost identical: approximately +0.57 vs +0.59 pp. Removing tiny gaps did not eliminate the effect; ≥0.10 ATR and ≥0.20 ATR filters remained around +0.70 pp.

Conclusion

FVG status adds some incremental information, but distance and market state explain much more. This supports using FVG as one feature in a larger model rather than as the main explanatory variable.
