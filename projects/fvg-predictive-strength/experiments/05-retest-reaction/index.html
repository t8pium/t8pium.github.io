Experiment 05 / Support-resistance behavior

First-touch retest reaction

Once price returns to an FVG, does it react differently from a comparable ordinary zone?

Hypothesis

An FVG may be weak as a magnet yet still matter as a temporary support/resistance region. This experiment ignores whether price was “attracted” there and starts the test only after a retest occurs.

Construction

  1. Wait for price to reach the near edge of a real FVG or matched control zone.
  2. Define a rejection target one full zone width away from the zone.
  3. Race that rejection target against traversal through the full zone.
  4. Repeat with matched ordinary zones under similar market state.

A secondary measure tracked average movement away from the zone over the next three bars, normalized by ATR.

Movement away

Mean three-bar movement away from the zone was also positive relative to control on every tested timeframe: approximately +0.015 ATR on 1m, +0.014 on 5m, +0.017 on 15m, +0.025 on 1H, and +0.059 on 4H.

Conclusion

The reaction effect is small, but its sign is more consistent across timeframes than the magnet test. That suggests the more stable question may be how price behaves at an FVG, not whether an FVG permanently pulls price toward itself.
