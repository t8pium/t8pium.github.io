Experiment 01 / Baseline behavior

Raw fill rates

The first experiment measures what FVGs do without pretending that a high fill percentage automatically proves anything special.

Hypothesis

If FVGs are genuinely special magnets, their raw revisit rate should be high—but a valid conclusion also requires an ordinary-zone baseline.

Procedure

For the mechanically detected one-minute FVG population, future paths were checked for initial touch, midpoint mitigation, full fill, multiple fixed horizons, and eventual touch before the dataset ended. Right-censored events were not silently discarded.

Baseline problem

At roughly three trading days the matched control-zone touch rate was about 98.97%, slightly above the FVG rate of about 98.95%. By one trading day both were roughly 98.2%.

Result: “Almost every FVG eventually fills” is descriptively true and inferentially weak. MNQ revisits nearby prices extremely often.

Conclusion

Raw fill rate is not a useful proof of FVG-specific attraction. The rest of the study therefore focuses on incremental effects versus controls, not the headline percentage.
