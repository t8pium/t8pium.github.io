Experiment 03 / Conditional survival

FVG age decay

If an FVG survives unfilled, does the label still add predictive value later?

Construction

Only zones that had survived to a given age were retained. The experiment then measured touch probability over the next interval for FVG survivors versus control survivors.

1m result

ConditionIncremental FVG effect
Survived 1 bar → touch by bar 3+3.09 pp
Survived 3 → touch by 5−0.21 pp
Survived 5 → touch by 10−0.08 pp
Survived 10 → touch by 20−1.86 pp

Other timeframes

  • 5m: +2.39 pp after surviving 1 bar, then much smaller / mixed later.
  • 15m: +1.85 pp early, ~0 after 3 bars, negative at older ages.
  • 1H: +1.43 pp early, then negative after several bars.

Interpretation

The effect is concentrated near creation. An old unfilled FVG does not behave like a constant market “obligation.” The evidence is more consistent with a temporary information state that gets absorbed.

Conclusion

Freshness matters. Treating a newly formed gap and an old surviving gap as equivalent loses one of the clearest patterns in the data.
